Sustainable architecture · Circular construction

Buildings that stay useful, and materials that come back.

SustainArc works on sustainable architecture and circular construction: designing for long life, adaptation and reuse, and making material and climate choices measurable from the first sketch.

Discuss a project What we offer
Focus

Four areas we work in

  • Circularity

    Design for disassembly and reuse, with the material documentation that makes reuse possible later.

  • Climate and life cycle

    Emissions assessed over the whole life of the building, and decided early, when choices are still cheap to change.

  • Digital workflow

    BIM models that carry quantities and material data, as a basis for life-cycle analysis and digital twins.

  • Local grounding

    Building tradition, climate and locally available materials taken seriously as design inputs.
